# Sparedût

A browser first-person shooter set in Friuli, where two folklore teams fight over a map. This glossary fixes the words used for rooms, matches and the things in them; the interface shows them in Friulian and Italian.

## Language

### Rooms and matches

**Room**:
A place players gather to play, with its code, settings, host and members. It is what appears in the room list.
_Italian_: Stanza
_Avoid_: Partita (when meaning the place), lobby, game, server

**Match**:
One round played inside a room, from its start to its end. A room hosts many matches in a row.
_Italian_: Partita
_Avoid_: Game, round

**Muster**:
The phase of a room before a match starts, where players pick a team and a primary and the host starts the match. Nobody is on the map during it.
_Italian_: Adunata
_Avoid_: Warmup, riscaldamento, lobby, stanza di preparazione, ready room

**Room list**:
The rooms a player can reach, shown before joining.
_Avoid_: Server browser, LAN list

**Reach**:
Who can see and join a room: **Offline** (only the player who created it, against bots), **LAN** (only players on the same network) or **Online** (anyone).
_Italian_: Portata
_Avoid_: Visibility, public/private, solo, single player, quick play

**Access**:
Whether a room lets anyone within its reach in (**Open**) or asks for a password (**Password**).
_Italian_: Accesso
_Avoid_: Closed, locked, private

**Single weapon**:
A room setting that gives every player the same primary, chosen by the host, and takes away the rest of what they carry except the melee weapon. It is either off or one of the primaries.
_Italian_: Arma unica
_Avoid_: Single-weapon mode, modalità arma singola, weapon lock

### On a device

**Installed game**:
The game added to a device's home screen and opened from its icon, without the browser's bars, rather than from a browser tab.
_Italian_: App installata
_Avoid_: PWA, web app, native app

**No network**:
The device has no connection at all. The game still opens once it has been opened online, and only Offline rooms and the training can be played. It is a state of the device, not a reach.
_Italian_: Senza rete
_Avoid_: Offline (when meaning the device's connection)

### Training

**Training**:
A guided session for one player that teaches the controls, the weapons and the modes. It is always offline and never appears in the room list.
Its mode is **Dutrine** and its map **Sagre dal Frico**, both provisional names.
_Italian_: Allenamento
_Avoid_: Tutorial, bonus level, practice

**Station**:
One stop of the training, teaching a single topic such as a weapon or a mode.
_Italian_: Stazione
_Avoid_: Stage, level, chapter

**Step**:
A single action the player must perform at a station before the training moves on.
_Italian_: Passo
_Avoid_: Task, objective, hint

### Armoury

**Primary**:
The weapon a player chooses for a match. It cannot be changed while the match is on.
_Italian_: Arma principale
_Avoid_: Main weapon, loadout

**Class**:
What a player is in a match, set by their primary: it fixes their health, their speed and any trait, such as the medic's healing.
_Italian_: Classe
_Avoid_: Role, character, hero

### World

**Map**:
A place a match is played in, with its geometry, spawns and objectives.
_Italian_: Mappa
_Avoid_: Level, livello, arena

**Mode**:
The rules that decide how a match is scored and won.
_Italian_: Modalità
_Avoid_: Game type

**Turret**:
A fixed emplacement on a map that a player mounts to use its gun.
_Italian_: Torretta
_Avoid_: Machine gun, mitragliatrice (when meaning the emplacement)

**Mitraie**:
The gun on a turret. It cannot be carried.
_Avoid_: Turret (when meaning the gun), machine gun

**Objective**:
What a match is played for, in force one at a time: none, the frico, the plaçute, the cjar or the storm. A mode puts its own in force; the training changes it from station to station.
_Italian_: Obiettivo
