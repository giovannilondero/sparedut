Spec #72 · ticket #77 · Muster prototypes

Adunade

Three throwaway layouts of the Muster with the same content: the two teams with each member's primary and ready mark, team and primary with health and speed, the room's mode, map, length, reach, access and code, the chat, the host's bot tools and single weapon, start and the countdown. Each has four screens: the Muster as its host sees it, the countdown, a player joining a match already on (score, time, confirm), and a guest in a single-weapon room (picker locked). The small white bar in a corner switches variant, screen and language; it is not part of the design. Look at a phone-sized window, upright and on its side, before the desktop. The Motosee is in the picker as a seventh primary, to lay out seven cards.
