Issue #93 · next primary prototypes

Prossime vite

A player asks for another primary while a match is on; they keep the one in hand until they die, and come back with the new one and its class. Three throwaway designs answer two questions together: where the player chooses, and how they are told that a change is waiting. Each has the same screens: the match menu with nothing asked for, the menu just after asking, the HUD during the life, the death screen, and a single-weapon room. The HUD and the match menu are the game's own, drawn with fixed data over a still of Glemone. The white bar at the top switches variant, screen, single weapon, device and language; it is not part of the design. Look at a phone-sized window, upright and on its side, before the desktop. New texts are in both languages; the Friulian is generated, the owner's to correct.
